How to use StaySync

1

Reading the calendar

Each day shows its nightly rate and status by colour: green = available, red = booked (with the guest's name), amber = blocked. Friday and Saturday nights carry a +{{ weekendUpliftPercent() }}% weekend surcharge by default — you'll see it baked into the rate shown.

2

Selecting a range

Click a day to start a selection, then click another day (or the same day again) to complete the range — it highlights blue. This range feeds the Rate & availability card below, and also auto-fills the Check-in / Check-out fields in the New booking card, so you don't have to retype dates you just clicked.

3

Setting a rate or blocking dates

With a range selected, enter a nightly rate and click Set rate to override it for those days — an override always wins over the weekend surcharge. Click Block to take those days off the market, or Unblock to make blocked days available again. Blocking a day that's already booked is rejected.

4

Creating a booking

Check-in and check-out follow the standard hospitality convention: check-out is exclusive, so a stay from the 10th to the 12th books 2 nights (the 10th and 11th), and a new guest could check in on the 12th the same day. A clashing date range is rejected gracefully with a banner, never a raw error. Stays that include a Friday or Saturday night need a minimum of {{ minStayNights() }} nights; a pure weeknight stay has no minimum.

5

Importing the channel feed

Click Import mock feed to reconcile a mock OTA/channel reservation feed into the calendar. The summary afterwards breaks down what happened: Imported (newly booked), Duplicates in feed (the same reservation appeared twice), Already imported (you've run this import before — safe to re-run any time), Cancelled (skipped), and Conflicts (a reservation that clashed with another booking or block, with the reason listed).

6

Spotting bookings at a glance

The Bookings this month list under the calendar grid lists every booking in the visible month — guest, date range, and number of nights — so you don't have to scan every day cell to see who's staying when. Click Cancel on a row to delete that booking and free its nights back to available (you'll be asked to confirm first).
